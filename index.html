CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

A repeatable way to learn the game.

This is this site's recommended learning model, not an official sequence or a promise of results. It organizes confirmed market, order, portfolio, P&L, level, bot, and rebirth systems into a manageable loop.

  1. 01
    Screen

    Screen a market

    Choose a stock, ETF, future, or IPO category to observe.

  2. 02
    Research

    Read the available context

    Check price context, indicators, and order flow where the current interface makes them available.

  3. 03
    Plan

    Choose Long or Short and risk settings

    Only after you can explain the order and the visible risk fields, consider a position.

  4. 04
    Manage

    Watch Positions and Orders

    Use the portfolio views to see what is open instead of stacking decisions blindly.

  5. 05
    Review

    Review the P&L

    Use the P&L calendar and history to identify what you learned from the result.

  6. 06
    Progress

    Add progression systems later

    Levels, bots, and rebirths can come after a repeatable review habit—not before it.

COMMON MISTAKES

Slow down before the next click.

These are practical safeguards for learning a simulation. They do not predict how any trade, bot, or rebirth will perform.

  • 01Using high leverage or a large position before understanding the visible trade fields.
  • 02Skipping the Fee and Liquidation Price fields when they are shown in the trading panel.
  • 03Opening a position without understanding the Stop Loss control available to it.
  • 04Treating a simulated market as real-world investing or financial advice.
  • 05Never checking positions, history, or the P&L calendar after a trade.
  • 06Guessing rebirth requirements, costs, resets, or rewards that this guide has not verified.
