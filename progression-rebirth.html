PROGRESSION / VERIFIED MILESTONES

Build knowledge before chasing milestones.

The official description confirms level ups, rebirths, algorithmic bots, portfolio growth while offline, and a P&L calendar. The official badge list provides a few visible milestones too.

10 badgesVerified public badge names
01

Levels and rebirths

Both are confirmed game systems, and First Rebirth is an official badge. We do not state the requirements, cost, reset effect, or reward because those details are not verified here.

02

Algo bots and offline growth

Algorithmic bots and offline portfolio growth are named in the official description. Their availability, returns, and exact behavior can vary in-game, so none are promised by this guide.

03

Milestones as a checklist

Use badges as a record of systems you have met, rather than as a reason to rush. First Short and First Futures Trade indicate that those paths exist; they are not beginner requirements.
