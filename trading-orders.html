CONFIRMED ORDER TOOLS

Know the control before you use it.

Roblox Stock Exchange 2 lists limit orders, stop losses, take profits, live order flow, and advanced indicators. Here is a simple way to encounter them without inventing settings or outcomes.

4 toolsConfirmed by the official description
01

Limit orders

A confirmed system for setting an order condition. Use the in-game interface to check the choices available to you; this guide does not assume any specific values or fills.

02

Stop losses

A confirmed risk-control tool. Learn what it displays in your current position before you rely on it as an automatic answer.

03

Take profits

A confirmed exit-control tool. Pair it with a clear reason for the trade rather than treating it as a profit guarantee.

04

Live order flow

The official description lists live order flow and advanced indicators. Read the interface slowly and avoid treating a single signal as certainty.

INTERFACE CHECKLIST

Labels you can actually look for.

Current interface seen in captured gameplay includes Trade, Research, Empire, Portfolio, Available Cash, and Level. In the trading panel, Long/Short, Market/Limit, Margin, Take Profit, Stop Loss, Leverage, Fee, and Liq price (liquidation price) are visible labels. Positions, Orders, Flow, P&L, Feed, History, and News are also present.

STAY METHODICAL

Suggested learning order

  1. Understand the basic market categories from the Beginner Guide.
  2. Observe a regular trade and its portfolio result.
  3. Read the order controls, then try one only when you can explain its purpose.
  4. Leave leverage and shorting until after you understand the order tools.

This order is a learning recommendation from this site, not an official sequence or a claim about unlocked features.
